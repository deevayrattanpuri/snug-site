Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Snug resizes photos on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded, and Snug has no account to sign up for. This page explains exactly what that means, and the one place where data does leave your device.

The short version

  • Your photos never leave your phone. Every resize, crop, conversion and PDF is done on the device. Snug works in airplane mode.
  • There is no account. No sign-up, no login, no email address. Snug never asks who you are and stores nothing about you.
  • We run ads, through Google AdMob. That is the one part of the app that talks to the internet, and it is covered in detail below.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs. Snug does not measure how you use it.

Your photos and files

Snug asks for access to your photos so you can choose one to work on, and so it can save the result. Both happen entirely on your device.

  • Photos are read from your gallery only when you pick one.
  • Results are saved as new files in Pictures/Snug. Your originals are left exactly as they were.
  • Replacing an original is optional and always asks first.
  • Snug has no server. There is nothing to upload a photo to, and no account it could be attached to.
  • If you use the camera inside Snug, the photo is handled the same way: processed on the device, saved to your gallery.

If a photo carries location data in its EXIF metadata, that data stays on your device too. Snug can strip it for you — that is what the Metadata tool does — but it never reads it anywhere else.

Advertising

Snug is free and paid for by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which is a separate company processing data under its own policies.

To show an ad, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, a resettable identifier Android provides for this purpose;
  • device and network information, such as device model, OS version, language and approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ads shown to you, such as whether one was viewed or tapped.

Snug never sends your photos, or anything derived from them, to AdMob or anyone else. The ad SDK has no access to your gallery.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Snug shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and your choice is respected. If you decline personalised ads, you still see ads — they are simply not personalised. You can change your mind later from the app's settings.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Paying to remove ads

Going ad-free is an optional subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing, using the Google account already signed in on your phone.

  • Snug never sees your card, your billing address or your name.
  • Snug receives only one thing back from Play: whether an active subscription exists.
  • Because the purchase belongs to your Google Play account, it restores on any phone you sign into with that account. That is Play's account, not a Snug one.

Paying removes ads and nothing else. Every tool, preset, batch size and output stays free whether you pay or not.

Permissions, and why each is asked for

PermissionWhy
Photos and mediaTo let you pick a photo, and to save the result into Pictures/Snug. Required.
CameraOnly if you take a photo inside Snug, for passport, document or form sizes. Optional — decline it and everything else still works.
InternetUsed only to fetch ads and to talk to Google Play. Never to send your photos.

Snug does not ask for contacts, microphone, precise location, or your files outside the gallery.

Children

Snug is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will look into it — though as the app has no account and no server, there is generally nothing held to delete.

Your rights

Because Snug holds no account and stores nothing about you on any server, there is no personal data for us to export, correct or delete on request. Everything Snug keeps — your settings, your job history and your photos — lives on your own device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

For the advertising data described above, Google is the controller. You can exercise your rights over it through your Google account and the Android ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the updated date at the top will change and the new version will be published here before it takes effect.
